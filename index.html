The problem

Most company AI treats everyone the same.

Nothing sits between your people, your documents and your AI. That missing layer is the knowledge control plane.

Connect an AI to your shared files and two things go wrong. Anyone can ask about any document, even ones they shouldn't see. And your documents can be sent to whatever outside AI service or plug-in the AI uses. For defense and aircraft maintenance companies, either mistake can mean a security breach, an export-law violation or a broken customer contract.

Security spills

Sensitive government information shown to an employee who isn't cleared.

Export-law violations

Export-controlled technical data shown to someone not authorized.

Contract breaches

One customer's data used to answer another customer's question.

Silent leaks

Restricted data sent to an outside AI service or app nobody approved.

01 / How it works

How the Knowledge Control Plane works.

The control plane has two halves. Control: connect, label and set the rules once. Enforcement: check, limit and test on every question.

01Control

Connect

Reads who's allowed to see what from the systems you already use: your user directory (Entra ID / Active Directory), SharePoint and shared folders. Nothing to rebuild.

02Control

Label

Tags documents by sensitivity and owner: government-controlled, export-controlled, program, customer, contract.

03Control

Set rules

Admins add roles and walled-off groups of documents ("silos") when your existing systems don't cover a rule.

04Enforcement

Check first

Before the AI looks at anything, SafeSilo narrows it to what that person may see. The AI never sees the rest. Every answer cites only allowed sources, and every question is logged.

05Enforcement

Control exits

Every outside AI service or tool gets a sensitivity limit: Public, Internal or Restricted. Data above that limit never goes there.

06Enforcement

Test run first

Start in watch-only mode. SafeSilo maps every path and records what it would block, without disrupting anyone. Switch on enforcement when you're ready.

05 / FAQ

Questions.

What is a knowledge control plane?

The layer between your people, your documents and your AI. It decides who each answer can draw from and which AI tools may receive your data, and it logs every question.

How is this different from Copilot or Glean?

Those are AI apps that run in someone else's cloud and rely on a copy of your permissions. SafeSilo is the control plane underneath: it runs on your hardware and checks permissions before the AI sees any content.

Do we have to rebuild our permissions?

No. It reads your existing groups and permissions. You only add rules where your current systems fall short.

What happens when someone's access changes?

Access updates on a schedule you set (default: near-real-time for changes in your user directory). Once someone's access is removed, it's gone as of their next question.

Can it run with no internet connection?

Yes. It can run fully disconnected, with updates delivered offline. If you do allow outside AI services or tools, SafeSilo controls exactly which data can reach them.

Can we prove it to an auditor?

Every question is logged: who asked, their role, which sources were used and which were kept out. Export it for audits.

Can our team still use cloud models or tools?

Yes, where policy allows. Each outside tool has a sensitivity limit; data above it stays home.

Will turning this on break existing workflows?

No. Start in watch-only mode: everything keeps working and every would-be block is recorded. Turn on enforcement when the map looks right.
